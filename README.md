# Better Nether 9minecraft Mod - Survive Forever in the Nether

Better Nether 9minecraft Mod turns the fiery dimension into a massive world filled with life. It adds over twenty new biomes, strange creatures, and hidden structures to explore. You get new crops to farm, materials to mine, and gear to craft directly in the underworld. This mod gives you everything needed to survive and live permanently below the surface without ever returning to the overworld.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Team BetterX**'s original work, kept running on current releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Loader: Fabric.
